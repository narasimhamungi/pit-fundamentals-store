# AAPL PIT Walkthrough

Use `pit-fundamentals aapl-demo` to generate the deterministic result in `outputs/aapl_pit_demo.txt`.

The example is based on Apple's real 2009 10-K and 2010 10-K/A. The original filing reported $5.704bn net income; the amendment reported $8.235bn after retrospective adoption of revised revenue-recognition accounting.
